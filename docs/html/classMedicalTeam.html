Detailed Description

Concrete Leaf managing medical triage, supply tracking, and patient admissions.

GoF Role: ConcreteObserver (Observer Pattern) / Leaf (Composite Pattern). Remains operational during emergency alerts and evacuations to provide first aid support[cite: 1].
