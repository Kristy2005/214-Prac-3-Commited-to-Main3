\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Concrete Leaf managing primary festival entry and age verification. 

GoF Role\+: Concrete\+Observer (\mbox{\hyperlink{classObserver}{Observer}} Pattern) / Leaf (Composite Pattern). Controls access at venue boundaries and halts admissions during closure or evacuation notices\mbox{[}cite\+: 1\mbox{]}.
